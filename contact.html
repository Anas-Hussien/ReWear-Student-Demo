Quick answers

Before you write.

Is ReWear a real marketplace?

Not yet — this is a student project exploring what a warmer, more sustainable second-hand shop could look like. Orders and accounts are simulated.

How do payments work?

They don't. There's no real checkout, no card details, and no money moves. Everything you see is a demo.

Can I return something?

Since no real orders are placed, there's nothing to return. In a live version, we'd offer a simple return window and clear condition guarantees.

Something's broken — where do I report it?

Use the form on this page. Describe what happened and which page you were on. Screenshots help too.
