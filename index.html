How it works

Honestly? It's just passing something on.

No spreadsheets. No confusing shipping labels. Three small steps — and a piece you loved gets another chapter with someone who'll love it too.

01

Snap & list

Got something you're ready to let go? Take a few photos, tell its story, set your price. Listing takes less time than folding laundry.

02

Discover

Search by size, style, or mood. Every listing comes from a real person — not a warehouse — so you know what you're getting.

03

Wear it, then pass it on

When you're done, list it again. The best clothes don't retire. They keep moving.
