About ReWear

Good clothes deserve more than one story.

ReWear started with a question that wouldn't leave us alone: why do we treat beautiful, wearable clothing as if it's disposable? We didn't have a grand answer — just a small idea that felt worth trying. Make it easier for people to pass on the clothes they've loved, and to find the ones that will love them next.

Buy thoughtfully

Browse by category, size, or price. Save what you love. Buy only what you'll actually wear — that's the whole point.

Sell simply

An honest description and a couple of clear photos is all it takes. No spreadsheets. No shipping labels. No fees.

Built as a demo

This is a student project — orders, accounts, and payments are simulated. But the mission behind it isn't hypothetical: good clothes really do deserve a second chance.

What we believe

Four small ideas we keep coming back to.

🧵

Clothes carry stories

A jacket holds the night you wore it. The right owner should be able to add to that story — not start over.

🌍

Small acts, repeated

No single purchase saves the planet. A million small choices, made by ordinary people, actually might.

🤝

Honesty is the product

Real people, real descriptions, real prices. The moment any of that becomes fake, we've lost the point.
