In practice

Three small habits that actually move the needle.

1. Describe it honestly

Clear details about condition and wear help buyers choose pieces they'll actually keep wearing — not return or throw away. Honesty sells better than hype.

2. Buy what you'll really wear

Second-hand works best when an item fills a real gap and gets worn often. A “deal” you never touch isn't a deal — it's just clutter with a discount.

3. Pass it on

When you're done with a piece, list it or donate it. Keeping it in circulation is what gives it another life — and keeps it out of landfill.

What's a fair price for second-hand clothes in Egypt?

On Libsy, Egypt's main pre-loved marketplace, the median asking price is EGP 500. Most everyday pieces sell between EGP 200 and 400, and under-EGP-200 items have the highest sell-through rate at 36%.
